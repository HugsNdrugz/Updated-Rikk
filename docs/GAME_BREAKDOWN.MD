# My Nigga Rikk - Game Breakdown

## 1. Introduction & Core Game Concept
    - **Overview:** "My Nigga Rikk" is a single-player, menu-driven simulation game with a street hustle theme. The player takes on the role of Rikk, engaging in transactions with various customers.
    - **Core Player Objective:** The primary goal appears to be to complete a set number of customer interactions (`MAX_FIENDS`) while managing resources (Cash), risk (Heat), and potentially building reputation (Street Cred). Success is determined by the player's financial state and possibly their reputation at the end of the game.
    - **Theme and Setting:** The game is set in an urban, street environment. Dialogue and item names ("Green Crack", "White Pony", "Fiends") reinforce a gritty, illicit trade theme. Interactions primarily occur through a simulated phone interface.

## 2. Main Game Loop
    - **Progression:** The game progresses in turns, each turn typically representing an interaction with a new customer ("fiend"). The total number of turns is limited by `MAX_FIENDS` (config: 15 in `script.js`).
    - **Customer Interaction Sequence (`script.js#nextFiend`):**
        1.  **Turn Progression & Events (`script.js#handleTurnProgressionAndEvents`):**
            *   The day of the week advances (`GameState.advanceDayOfWeek`).
            *   Active world events have their durations decremented; new random world events may trigger (checking `isToolTriggered: true` to avoid re-triggering specific events).
            *   Player's Heat passively changes (decreases based on `lowProfile` skill, modified by world events, `info_cops` tool).
            *   Tool-triggered events (like "rival_stash_opportunity" from `info_rival` tool) are checked.
        2.  **New Interaction UI Setup (`script.js#setupUIForNewInteraction`):**
            *   Chat and choices are cleared via `UIManager`.
            *   "Next Customer" button is disabled.
            *   Phone UI is set to "docked."
            *   A "knock" sound and visual effect are displayed by `UIManager`.
        3.  **Customer Generation & Interaction Start (`script.js#generateAndStartCustomerInteraction`):**
            *   After a delay (`KNOCK_ANIMATION_DURATION`), a customer interaction is generated by `CustomerManager.js#generateInteraction`. This involves selecting/creating a customer instance (`_selectOrGenerateCustomerFromPool`) and determining their initial dialogue, what they might sell (via `_generateRandomItem`), or what they might want to buy from Rikk's inventory.
            *   The game then transitions to the chat view (`script.js#startCustomerInteraction`), displaying dialogue and then choices via `UIManager`.
    - **Player Choice & Deal Resolution (`script.js#handleChoice`):**
        *   Player makes a choice (buy, sell, negotiate, decline).
        *   The outcome is processed: cash/inventory changes in `GameState`.
        *   Stats (heat, cred) are updated based on item properties, customer `gameplayConfig`, and payloads.
        *   Addiction is processed for drug sales (`CustomerManager.processPotentialAddiction`).
        *   Relevant customer dialogue for the outcome is displayed.
        *   Payloads associated with choices or dialogue can trigger further effects (`script.js#processPayload` modifying stats or triggering sub-events like 'snitchReport').
    - **End of Interaction (`script.js#endCustomerInteraction`):**
        *   Choices are cleared, phone UI returns to home.
        *   "Next Customer" button is enabled if game conditions permit.
        *   Game state is saved (`saveGameState`).
    - **Game End Conditions (`script.js#endGame`):**
        *   Player successfully serves `MAX_FIENDS` customers: "completed" game verdict based on final cash.
        *   Player's Heat reaches `MAX_HEAT`: "heat" game over.
        *   Player's Cash is zero (or less) AND Inventory is empty: "bankrupt" game over.

## 3. Player Stats & Progression
    - **Cash (`GameState.cash`):**
        - **Earned/Spent:** Through deals, slot game.
        - **Impact:** Primary transaction resource. Depletion (if also out of items) ends the game. Final amount influences end-game verdict. Starting: `STARTING_CASH` (500).
    - **Heat (`GameState.heat`):**
        - **Gained:** From item properties (via `data_items.js`, applied in `CustomerManager` or `script.js#processPayload`), customer `gameplayConfig.heatImpact`, specific event payloads. Modified by world event `heatModifier` and `applyDealHeat` (which checks for `burner_phone`).
        - **Reduced:** Passively each turn in `script.js#handleTurnProgressionAndEvents` (base reduction increased by `lowProfile` skill and `info_cops` tool).
        - **Impact:** Reaching `MAX_HEAT` (100) leads to game over.
    - **Street Cred (`GameState.streetCred`):**
        - **Gained/Lost:** From customer `gameplayConfig.credImpactSell`/`credImpactBuy` (in `script.js#handleChoice`), specific event payloads, buying "nootropics_focus".
        - **Purpose:** Influences end-game verdict. No other explicit mechanical impact currently identified in the code. Starting: `STARTING_STREET_CRED` (0).
    - **Skills (`GameState.playerSkills`):**
        -   `negotiator`: Increases success chance of "negotiate_sell" option in `script.js#handleChoice`.
        -   `appraiser`: Adjusts `effectiveValue` of items in `CustomerManager.js#_calculateItemValue` (player pays less when buying, Rikk's perceived value is higher when selling).
        -   `lowProfile`: Increases passive heat reduction per turn in `script.js#handleTurnProgressionAndEvents`.
        - **Modification:** Skills are updated via `game.updatePlayerSkill()` in `script.js#processPayload` from dialogue/event payloads.
    - **Days/Turns:**
        - Tracked by `GameState.fiendsLeft` (counts down from `MAX_FIENDS`) and `GameState.dayOfWeek`.

## 4. Customer Interaction System
Managed by `classes/CustomerManager.js` using data from `data/customer_templates.js` and `data/data_items.js`.

-   **Customer Archetypes (`data/customer_templates.js`):**
    -   Defined by `key`, `baseName`, `avatarUrl`, `baseStats` (mood, loyalty, etc.).
    -   **`gameplayConfig`:** Dictates transactional behavior.
        -   `buyPreference` / `sellPreference`: Objects or `or` arrays defining item characteristics (type, subType, quality, id, value, exclude, chance for selling). `CustomerManager.js` filters items based on these. `{any: true/false}` flags general willingness.
        -   `priceToleranceFactor`: Modifies deal prices.
        -   `negotiationResists`: Boolean for haggling.
        -   `heatImpact`, `credImpactSell`/`Buy`: Affect player stats post-deal.
        -   `sellsOnly`, `itemPool`, `itemPoolWeird`, `chanceSellWeirdItem`: Control what customers offer. Oddity sales (from `itemPoolWeird` or general random oddity path) are now gated by high addiction status (`cravingLevel >= 4`) and `CONFIG.CHANCE_SELL_WEIRD_ITEM`.
-   **Dialogue System (`CustomerManager.js#_getDialogue`):**
    -   Uses context keys (e.g., `greeting`) linked to arrays of dialogue blocks.
    -   Blocks are chosen based on `conditions` (customer stats like mood, addiction) evaluated by `_checkCondition`.
    -   Random line selected from chosen block; placeholders (`[CUSTOMER_NAME]`, `[ITEM_NAME]`) are filled.
    -   Optional `payload` triggers game effects via `script.js#processPayload`.
-   **Deal Mechanics:**
    -   `CustomerManager.js#generateInteraction`: Determines if customer sells (calls `_generateRandomItem`) or buys.
        -   If customer sells: `_calculateItemValue` determines their asking price.
        -   If customer buys: Filters Rikk's inventory (addiction, `buyPreference`, world events, then a low `CONFIG.CHANCE_CUSTOMER_BUYS_RANDOM_ITEM` for other items). `_calculateItemValue` determines Rikk's base sell price; customer's offer derived using their `priceToleranceFactor`.
    -   `script.js#handleChoice`: Processes player's decision, updates `GameState`, triggers addiction checks, applies stat changes, and queues outcome dialogue.
-   **Customer State & Management (`CustomerManager.js`):**
    -   `_selectOrGenerateCustomerFromPool`: Manages `customersPool`.
    -   Returning customers: Mood can be affected by `metadata.pendingMoodEffect`.
    -   New customers: Initialized with `baseStats`, default addiction status (`isAddicted: false`, `cravingLevel: 0`), and `metadata: {}`.
    -   `addictionStatus`: Tracks `isAddicted`, `drugId`, `cravingLevel`. `cravingLevel` influences price tolerance (for their drug) and chance to sell oddities.

## 5. Inventory & Item System
-   **Item Definitions (`data/data_items.js`):**
    -   `itemTypes` array: `id`, `name`, `baseValue`, `range` (used in `_calculateItemValue` for price fluctuation), `type` (DRUG, STOLEN_GOOD, INFORMATION, TOOL), `subType`, `heat`, `description`.
    -   `ITEM_QUALITY_LEVELS` (display names) & `ITEM_QUALITY_MODIFIERS` (price multipliers).
-   **Item Effects (Mechanical):**
    -   `addictionChance` (DRUG): Processed in `CustomerManager.js#processPotentialAddiction`.
    -   `effect` (TOOL/INFORMATION): String keys used by `GameState.isToolEffectActive()` to trigger benefits:
        -   "deal_heat_reduction" (`burner_phone`): Reduces heat from deals/payloads.
        -   "reduce_heat_small" (`info_cops`): Increases passive heat reduction.
        -   "rival_op_chance" (`info_rival`): Triggers "rival_stash_opportunity" world event.
    -   Descriptive `effects` on DRUGs (e.g., "jitters", "paranoia_high_dose", "improved_focus") have some linked mechanics:
        -   `paranoia_high_dose`: Chance to set `pendingMoodEffect` on customer.
        -   `jitters`: Adds +1 heat when sold.
        -   `improved_focus`: Chance for +1 Street Cred when Rikk buys it.
-   **Inventory (`GameState.inventory`):** Array of item objects, limited by `MAX_INVENTORY_SLOTS`.

## 6. Phone UI & Applications
Managed by `UIManager.js`, `phone_ambient_ui.js`, and specific app managers.

-   **Ambient UI (`phone_ambient_ui.js`):** Time, date, notifications, cosmetic battery/wallpaper.
-   **Phone States (`UIManager.js#setPhoneUIState`):** `docked`, `home`, `chatting`, `contacts`, `slots`, `theme-settings`, `offscreen`.
-   **Implemented Apps:**
    -   Messages: Core customer interaction chat.
    -   Inventory: Modal view of Rikk's stash.
    -   Contacts (`ContactsAppManager.js`): View/edit basic customer template info; create new templates. Search/More Options buttons are placeholders.
    -   Slots (`SlotGameManager.js`): Minigame using game cash. Relies on external CDNs for `TWEEN.js`, `html-table-builder.js`, Bootstrap, and remote image assets.
    -   Theme Settings (Phone): Customize phone CSS variables.
-   **Placeholder Applications (`script.js#handlePhoneAppClick`):** Music, Clock, Dialer, User Profile, Compass show "coming soon" notifications.

## 7. World Event System (`data/data_events.js`, `script.js`)
-   `possibleWorldEvents`: Array of event objects (`id`, `name`, `description`, `duration`, `effects` object).
-   **Triggering:**
    -   Random events in `script.js#handleTurnProgressionAndEvents` (filters out `isToolTriggered` events, aims for one random event at a time).
    -   Tool-triggered: "rival_stash_opportunity" via `info_rival`.
-   **Effects (`getCombinedActiveEventEffects`):** Modifiers like `heatModifier`, `customerScareChance`, `drugPriceModifier`, etc., are applied in `script.js` and `CustomerManager.js`.

## 8. Game State Management (`GameState.js`, `script.js`)
-   **Initialization:** `initGame` calls `uiManager.initDOMReferences`, `initializeManagers`, `initializeUIAndSettings`, `setupEventListeners`. `initializeNewGameState` resets `GameState` and `CustomerManager`.
-   **Save/Load:**
    -   `saveGameState()`: Saves `GameState` (including `customerManager` state) to localStorage via `SAVE_KEY`.
    -   `loadGameState()`: Loads from localStorage.
    -   `saveCustomerTemplates()` / `loadCustomerTemplates()`: Separate persistence for customer templates via `CUSTOMER_TEMPLATES_SAVE_KEY`.
    -   `isLocalStorageAvailable()` from `utils.js` checks browser support.

## 9. UI Management & Style Customization (`UIManager.js`, `index.html`, CSS)
-   `UIManager.js`: Handles all DOM references, HUD updates (`cash`, `day`, `heat`, `cred`, `eventTicker`), screen transitions, modal displays, phone UI state and content rendering (chat, choices).
-   **Style Customization:**
    -   Main Settings Panel (`#settings-menu-panel`): Allows customization of global CSS variables (colors, fonts, radii, spacing) via color pickers, selects, and range sliders.
    -   Phone Theme Settings (`#phone-theme-settings-view`): Similar, for phone-specific CSS variables.
    -   `UIManager` handles control initialization, loading/saving settings from localStorage (`STYLE_SETTINGS_KEY`), applying styles, and preview mode.

## 10. Data Flow Overview (Simplified)
-   **Configuration Data:** `data_items.js`, `data_customer_templates.js`, `data_events.js` provide static definitions.
-   **Runtime State:** `GameState.js` is the central store for dynamic data (player stats, inventory, active events, current customer instance, modified customer templates).
-   **Controller:** `script.js` orchestrates game flow, handles events, and coordinates between `GameState`, `UIManager`, and other managers.
-   **Customer Logic:** `CustomerManager.js` uses `GameState` (inventory, skills) and its own copy of `customerTemplates` to generate interactions.
-   **UI:** `UIManager.js` reads from `GameState` to display information and receives user input to pass to `script.js`. `ContactsAppManager.js` modifies a copy of customer templates and dispatches an event for `script.js` to update the main `GameState`.

## 11. Potential Areas for Future Expansion (User Brainstorm Summary)
The game has a solid foundation for significant expansion. Key areas identified by user feedback for future development include:
    -   **Deeper Customer Interactions:** More archetypes (undercover cops, regulars with loyalty, suppliers), dynamic dialogue based on game state/history, enhanced negotiation (bluffing, intimidation, bundling).
    -   **Expanded Item Dynamics:** Item quality/purity with mechanical effects, special/rare items (favors, tools), multiple stash spots with risk, item degradation, simple crafting/mixing.
    -   **Richer World Events:** More varied police/rival activity, neighborhood events, active informants/rumors, time of day/week effects.
    -   **Enhanced Player Progression:** More impactful reputation (Street Cred/Notoriety), more skills/perks, safehouse upgrades.
    -   **Complex Scenarios:** Moral dilemmas, betrayals, setups, unexpected opportunities, chain events, strategic heat management choices.
    -   **UI/Presentation:** Abstract neighborhood map, contact/event logs.
